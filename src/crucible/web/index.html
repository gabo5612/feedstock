Detail

Where the price sits relative to its own history, and — what matters — what happened afterwards the previous times it sat there, measured over 10 years. This is not a forecast.

Alerts

They run locally, with no external service. A rule that cannot be evaluated says no data, not "ok": painting green over what could not be measured gives confidence where there is no information.

Data quality

Freshness, gaps, name drift and outliers. Bad data nobody looks at is worse than missing data: the missing kind gets noticed, the bad kind gets used.

Correlation of daily returns, not prices: two trending series correlate highly even when unrelated. If copper and energy move together, hedging one does not hedge the other.
